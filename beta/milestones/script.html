Phylarium, scene by scene

Each scene opens a new world just before its change, so you can watch it happen. Scene 0 is a fresh world: the start.

  1. 1. The fifth star

    See: The dark after the Big Bang. Four tapped stars shine; one more twinkles somewhere.

    Play: Tap the twinkling star.

    Then: The stars fly past at light speed, the planet comes closer and the stars light up as it arrives. Visible light, Starlight and Magma are known.

  2. 2. The dark sun

    See: The planet in the dark, the stars out, the sun a dark disc.

    Play: Tap the sun again and again until its core lights.

    Then: Ignition: the sun shines at full light and the planet glows red.

  3. 3. The spin

    See: The lit planet. It can be spun by swiping.

    Play: Swipe the planet to spin it faster and faster.

    Then: The moon flies out and the spin locks. Rotation is known; the Magnetic Field follows the crust.

  4. 4. The crust

    See: The moon in the sky; the molten surface cooling.

    Play: Wait; the first tap on the moon finds its phase.

    Then: The crust sets and the first crack glows on it.

  5. 5. The first crack

    See: A crack glows somewhere on the crust.

    Play: Tap the crack.

    Then: A volcano cone rises where it glowed.

  6. 6. The first eruption

    See: A cone stands on the planet.

    Play: Tap the cone twice.

    Then: It rumbles, then erupts: lava, ash, gas, and the land grows round it.

  7. 7. Volcanoes

    See: Cones and cracks.

    Play: Erupt three times.

    Then: Restless Earth opens and Volcanoes is known; eruptions bring Volcanic Gas and Eruptions.

  8. 8. The first comet

    See: Night. A comet crosses the sky.

    Play: Tap the comet.

    Then: It crashes into the planet; its card is found, Comets is known and the basins start to fill.

  9. 9. The thaw

    See: A frozen planet; ice spots show where it can be chipped.

    Play: Tap the ice.

    Then: The ice melts into the first ocean.

  10. 10. Clouds

    See: The ocean under the sun; a sun ray can be dragged onto the water.

    Play: Drag a sun ray onto open water, a few times.

    Then: Evaporation, then the first cloud plops out and Clouds is known; from then on the sun steams clouds up by itself.

  11. 11. The first storm cloud

    See: Clouds drifting round the planet.

    Play: Watch two clouds meet.

    Then: They merge into a storm cloud, which rains, snows and strikes by itself.

  12. 12. Tides

    See: The moon over the sea at night.

    Play: Drag the moon across the sky.

    Then: The sea bulges under it and lets go as a wave; tidal pools are left on the shore. From then on the tides roll in by themselves.

  13. 13. The soup

    See: Storm clouds and tidal pools.

    Play: Tap a storm cloud three times over a pool, or wait for its own strike.

    Then: Lightning into a pool makes the soup of life; the Seas screen shows it build.

  14. 14. Life

    See: The soup nearly full.

    Play: Keep striking pools.

    Then: The first cell; the Biology page opens and the species evolve one from another.

  15. 15. Land

    See: Sea life; oxygen building in the air.

    Play: Wait for the ozone layer.

    Then: Plants go ashore and the land greens.

  16. 16. Dinosaurs

    See: Green land, animals on it.

    Play: Evolve on.

    Then: The dinosaurs; each night a body comes until one is caught.

  17. 17. The asteroid

    See: Dinosaurs live; a body comes each night.

    Play: Tap the body.

    Then: The asteroid falls and ends their age; mammals and birds take over.

  18. 18. Lucy

    See: Mammals and birds after the impact.

    Play: Evolve on.

    Then: Walking Upright: Lucy.
