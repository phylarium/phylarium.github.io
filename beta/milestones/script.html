Phylarium, scene by scene

Each scene opens a new world just before its change, so you can watch it happen.

  1. 1. The Big Bang

    See: A dot in the dark, swelling with each tap and shrinking back when the taps stop.

    Play: Tap the dot again and again.

    Then: The bang: a flash, then the dark, and somewhere in it one star twinkles.

  2. 2. Twinkle

    See: The dark after the Big Bang. One star twinkles somewhere; each tapped star stays lit.

    Play: Tap the twinkling star, five times in all.

    Then: The stars fly past at light speed, the planet comes closer and the stars light up as it arrives. Visible light, Starlight and Magma are known.

  3. 3. Sunrise

    See: The planet in the dark, the stars out, and a warm light glowing behind the top of the planet.

    Play: Tap the light, five times in all.

    Then: Each tap lifts the sun until it stands in the sky, yellow and without a face. Sunrise is known; Yellow is found.

  4. 4. The faceless sun

    See: The risen sun in the sky, yellow, without a face.

    Play: Tap the sun again and again until its core lights.

    Then: Ignition: the face plops out with a smile, the sun shines at full light and the planet glows red. Orange and Red are found.

  5. 5. The spin

    See: The lit planet, with Magma and Rotation developed. It can be spun by swiping.

    Play: Swipe the planet to spin it faster and faster.

    Then: The moon flies out and the spin locks; days begin. Crust can be developed.

  6. 6. The crust

    See: The moon in the sky; Crust developed, the molten surface cooling.

    Play: Wait; the first tap on the moon finds its phase.

    Then: The crust sets. Cracks can be developed.

  7. 7. Cracks

    See: The crust set; Cracks can be developed.

    Play: Develop Cracks, then Volcanoes.

    Then: The first crack glows on the crust.

  8. 8. The first crack

    See: A crack glows somewhere on the crust; Volcanoes developed.

    Play: Tap the crack.

    Then: A volcano cone rises where it glowed.

  9. 9. The first eruption

    See: Three cones stand on the planet; Volcanic Gas and Eruptions developed.

    Play: Tap a cone twice.

    Then: It rumbles, then erupts: lava, ash, gas, and the land grows round it.

  10. 10. The first comet

    See: Night, with Comets researched. A comet crosses the sky, its tail in the color of the ice it carries.

    Play: Tap the comet.

    Then: It crashes into the planet; its atom is found and the basins start to fill. Comets keep coming, one a night.

  11. 11. The thaw

    See: A frozen planet; ice spots show where it can be chipped.

    Play: Tap the ice.

    Then: The ice melts into the first ocean.

  12. 12. Clouds

    See: The ocean under the sun; Evaporation can be researched.

    Play: Research Evaporation, then tap the sun with the sea under it, a few times.

    Then: Each tap sends a ray that steams the sea; the first cloud plops out and Clouds is known; from then on the sun steams clouds up by itself.

  13. 13. The first storm cloud

    See: Clouds drifting round the planet.

    Play: Watch two clouds meet.

    Then: They merge into a storm cloud, which rains, snows and strikes by itself.

  14. 14. Tides

    See: The moon over the sea at night.

    Play: Drag the moon across the sky.

    Then: The sea bulges under it and lets go as a wave; tidal pools are left on the shore. From then on the tides roll in by themselves.

  15. 15. The soup

    See: Storm clouds and tidal pools.

    Play: Tap a storm cloud three times over a pool, or wait for its own strike.

    Then: Lightning into a pool makes the soup of life; the Seas screen shows it build.

  16. 16. Life

    See: The soup nearly full.

    Play: Keep striking pools.

    Then: The first cell; the Biology page opens and the species evolve one from another.

  17. 17. Land

    See: Sea life; oxygen building in the air.

    Play: Wait for the ozone layer.

    Then: Plants go ashore and the land greens.

  18. 18. Dinosaurs

    See: Green land, animals on it.

    Play: Evolve on.

    Then: The dinosaurs; each night a body comes until one is caught.

  19. 19. The asteroid

    See: Dinosaurs live; a body comes each night.

    Play: Tap the body.

    Then: The asteroid falls and ends their age; mammals and birds take over.

  20. 20. Lucy

    See: Mammals and birds after the impact.

    Play: Evolve on.

    Then: Walking Upright: Lucy.
